Before You Shop

How to Measure Yourself

For the most accurate fit, take your measurements with a soft measuring tape while wearing light-fitting undergarments. Keep the tape firm but not tight.

  • →Chest / Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • →Waist: Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso.
  • →Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 20–23cm below your waistline.
  • →Inseam: Measure from your crotch to the bottom of your ankle for trouser length.
  • →Foot Length: Stand on a piece of paper, trace your foot, and measure the longest distance for shoe sizing.
💬 Not sure about your size? Our styling team on WhatsApp (+237 653-603-986) can guide you to the perfect fit for any specific item in our collection — just share your measurements and we'll advise you directly.
